## Design Specificity Verdict

**The content layer is authored for PIA. The design layer is a swappable dark template with a green accent.**

Things no other airline could ship: a 192-frame render of a PIA-liveried 777 rotating off the runway; PK flight numbers; PKR fares lifted from the archived offers rail with `real: true` marking the verified ones; **Executive Economy** correctly named rather than inventing a "Business Class"; **Award +Plus**; Hajj & Umrah as top-level nav carrying the Zamzam allowance and the polio certificate; Sohni Dharti and *Humsafar*. That research is the best thing here.

The visual system does not carry it. The declared seven-stop ramp reaches the eye in three places, all under 6px tall — the footer rule, the boarding-pass stripe, the phone-mock bar. `--pia-indigo` and `--pia-teal` never render at all. Swap `--color-brand` for another carrier's green and nothing else would need to move.

**Deterministic scan**: `detect.mjs` exited 2 with **2 findings**, both `overused-font` (Inter) at `src/index.css:1` and `:46`. Thin on its own — but it independently corroborates the design-specificity verdict from a completely different direction.

**Visual overlays**: none. The overlay-injection flow was not run, so there is no user-visible highlight in a browser tab. Assessment B also could not launch Playwright's Chromium (the binary is absent from `~/.cache/ms-playwright`) and measured on Microsoft Edge 151 instead — Chromium-based, so layout and CSSOM results transfer.

## Overall Impression

The research is excellent, several components are genuinely beautiful, and the material system is more disciplined than most concept builds ever reach. All of it sits behind a door that does not open: 8.54 MiB of hero frames block first paint, and on mobile the booking form destroys itself the moment someone searches. The single biggest opportunity is not a redesign — it is unblocking what is already built.

## What's Working

**The offer tickets.** Boarding-pass structure where the typographic hierarchy maps to the decision order: what class → where → how much. The `PUBLISHED FARE` vs `ECONOMY` eyebrow tells you which numbers are real — the only honesty signal in the build, and a classy one.

**The departures board.** Deterministic split-flap locking left to right, JetBrains Mono with `tnum`, exactly one filled status pill among outlined ones so the eye lands on the flight actually leaving. The motion is the metaphor, and it is correctly gated under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**The material system.** Five classes carry the site: brighter top border than bottom, inset top highlight, `blur(24px) saturate(160%)`, and `.well` for fields pressed into the panel. Surfaces behave like glass over photography rather than flat cards.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The 192-frame preloader blocks the entire site.**
All 192 JPEGs must decode before `ready`; until then a `fixed inset-0 z-50` overlay covers everything. B measured all 192 fetched on first load of `/` — 8,954,940 bytes, no scrolling, no interaction. A measured 6% at 6s and 64% at 4m06s on a 1.6 Mbps profile.
*Why it matters:* this is the front door on the airline's primary market, where mobile connections routinely sit below 5 Mbps. No skip, no dismiss.
*Fix:* delete the gate. The 4K still already renders underneath at `PlaneScroll.jsx:179` — show the hero over it immediately, load frames lazily, draw the nearest decoded frame. Cut to ~48 frames with 4× skip; the scrub reads identically at 400vh.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable optimize`

**[P0] Search results make the booking form unreachable on mobile.**
The widget is `absolute inset-x-0 bottom-0` inside a `sticky h-screen overflow-hidden` container. When results render the panel grows upward past the container top and is clipped with no scroll. Verified at 390×844: tabs, trip type, From, To and Dates all gone and unrecoverable — recovery requires a reload, which re-triggers the preloader.
*Fix:* render results into a section below the hero and `scrollIntoView`, or make the widget static and in normal flow below `md`.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] `scroll-snap-type: y mandatory` hijacks scrolling and skips a booking widget.**
On `/destinations`, one scroll from the hero lands on the route map — the `FlightSearch` between them is not a snap target and is skipped entirely. `Section` uses `min-h-screen justify-center`, so taller sections push their H2 under the 130px fixed header.
*Fix:* `proximity` instead of `mandatory`, or scope snapping to the hero; add `scroll-margin-top: 8rem`; change `Section` to `justify-start pt-32`.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] No `<main>` landmark, and no `h1` on the homepage.**
B confirmed 0 `<main>` and 0 `[role="main"]` across all 8 routes, no skip link, and `/` starting at `h2` with no `h1` at all. Screen-reader users get no way to jump past a 130px header into content, and the homepage announces no title.
*Fix:* wrap `<Outlet />` in `<main id="content">`, add a skip link, promote the hero's first beat to `h1`.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Contrast fails on the controls that matter most.**
Both assessments converged on the same number from different methods: `text-white/40` on `#0a0e17` = **3.80:1**. That is every field label in the booking widget — FROM, TO, DATES, PASSENGERS, CABIN CLASS, CURRENCY — at 10px. Footer disclaimer 2.58:1; departures column headers 2.62:1; "Departure — Return" 3.12:1. 26 genuine failures on `/`, 17 on `/destinations`.
*Fix:* raise the field-label token to `white/65` (≈7.7:1); floor everything non-decorative at `white/55`.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable audit` follow-up, or `/impeccable polish`

**[P2] Zero error handling anywhere.**
`setFound(!!(pnr.trim() && ln.trim()))` — empty submit produces literal silence; garbage input ("XXXX"/"X") confidently returns `KHI → DXB · PK 213 · CONFIRMED`. Search returns four priced flights with no date entered. Every result row reads "2h 05m" — including Karachi to Toronto.
*Fix:* inline `role="alert"` under the PNR row; disable Search until a departure date exists and say why; derive duration from the sector.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable harden`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer)**: `Passengers (1 Adult)` is a bare text input whose label and placeholder are the identical string — an echo, not an instruction. "PNR number" with no explanation of what a PNR is. The **`Polio`** card sits third in the "Where you land" grid on `/hajj-umrah`, styled identically to `JED` and `MED` — three airports, one of which is a disease. `DestCard` renders a bare "62,500" with no currency.

**Riley (stress tester)**: every input is a valid booking. Switching currency to USD converts the results but leaves the offer tickets and destination cards in PKR — two currencies on one page. The mobile menu overlay is a later sibling in the same stacking context as the header pill, so it paints over its own close control, with no Escape handler and no focus trap. Home's `DestCard` shows Skardu at 20,830 from KHI — but 20,830 is the *Lahore* published fare.

**Casey (mobile)**: lost at second one to the preloader. Scroll-0 on 390×844 is entirely booking form — the aircraft he waited 8.54 MiB for is off-screen. The departures board clips its own Status column inside `panel overflow-hidden`: "STA…", "BOARDI…". The advisory bar truncates the polio notice, hides "Read more" below `sm`, and rotates away after 6s with no pause. `RouteMap` prints "HOVER A DESTINATION" on a touch device. 33 tap targets under 44px, including a 22×22 dismiss button and 15 footer links 17px tall.

## Minor Observations

- Favicon is still the old yellow→teal gradient mark while both shipped logos are PIA's green `#006937` / gold `#A48D29` identity.
- Ghost-word is `whitespace-nowrap` at 7rem, chopping mid-letter: "PA|" on `/about`, "MANAGE YOU|" on `/manage`.
- The hero story scrim is a hard-edged radius-less grey slab, inconsistent with the `.panel` glass everywhere else.
- `StickyScroll` prints the step title twice simultaneously on desktop and leaves ~600px of empty right column.
- Nine footer links plus the advisory "Read more" resolve to `#` — four of them are the exact help pages the body copy tells you to consult.
- `/manage` hero is a European departures board (Bucharest, Kiev, Sofia, Newcastle) on a Pakistani carrier. `/fleet` deliberately uses unmarked airframes, so "What we fly" contains zero PIA aircraft.
- Reduced motion: clouds, split-flap and scroll-reveal are all correctly gated — the hero canvas scrub and the `animate-pulse` LIVE dot are not.
- Footer logo is the one below-fold image without `loading="lazy"` on every route; one Unsplash asset ships at 3840×2560 for a 1440×900 slot.
- Genuinely worth keeping: `data.js` annotates which fares are verified and which are illustrative, and the comments explain why `motion.div` was avoided on React 19.2.

## Questions to Consider

1. You spent 8.54 MiB on the one asset no other airline could use, then put it behind a spinner that takes minutes on a Karachi mobile connection and scrubbed it away in the first 130px of desktop scroll. If the film is the argument, why is it the thing a real visitor is least likely to see?
2. Every surface here is a boarding pass, a departures board, or a PNR lookup — the language of a trip that already exists. What does the work of persuading someone who has not yet decided to go?
3. The stylesheet declares a seven-stop ramp, then ships one green and one lime, with indigo and teal never reaching a pixel. Is that a brand system, or a comment in a CSS file?
